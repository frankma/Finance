σ(K, T) across expiries with α, β, ρ and ν held constant, so T enters only through Hagan's time correction. The highlighted line is the current expiry. Drag or use the arrow keys to rotate; double-click to reset.

Implied Black vol smile

Hagan's lognormal expansion. The dashed line keeps α and β but sets ν = 0, so the gap between them is what stochastic vol adds.

Implied forward density

Breeden–Litzenberger: ∂²P/∂K² from Black put prices on the SABR smile. Red regions are negative density, i.e. butterfly arbitrage in the expansion.

Backbone

Smiles with α held fixed while F moves ±15%. The dashed ATM locus σ(K, K) is the backbone that β controls.

Normal (Bachelier) vol

Hagan's normal expansion against the Black smile converted to Bachelier vol through prices. Two expansions of one model; the gap is approximation error.
