    What the coach knows

    Your own cards, the board, the pot, the stacks and every bet made. Never another seat's cards or the order of the deck: the server never sends them to this page. Equity is estimated by dealing the other seats hands from the cards you have not seen, favouring hands that fit their public bets (in hold'em; in Omaha it deals them at random). The computer seats also see only their own cards, and do not read bets.
